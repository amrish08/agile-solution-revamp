Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

You may request cancellation of a service booking by contacting Agile Solution using the email address or phone number below.

1. Before work begins

Cancellation requests received before work has started will generally be accepted. If payment has already been collected, an eligible refund will be handled under our Refund Policy.

2. After work begins

If diagnosis, repair, installation or other agreed work has started, cancellation may be subject to charges for work completed and any approved parts or third-party costs already incurred. We will explain the calculation before closing the request, subject to applicable law.

3. Parts and special orders

If you have approved the purchase or ordering of a replacement part or special item, costs already committed for that item may be deducted where permitted by law. We will communicate this before ordering whenever possible.

4. How to cancel

Contact us as soon as possible with your name, phone number, service request and payment reference, if applicable. A cancellation is effective when Agile Solution confirms receipt of your request.

5. Our cancellation

If we need to cancel or cannot attend to a confirmed paid service, we will contact you to reschedule or arrange an eligible refund for the undelivered service.
