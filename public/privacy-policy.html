Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Agile Solution collects, uses and protects information submitted through this website, service enquiries and bookings.

1. Information we collect

When you enquire or book a service, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, device type, service selected and the issue description you choose to provide. Payment information is processed by PayU on its hosted payment pages; we do not ask you to share your card PIN, UPI PIN, password or OTP with us.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and confirm service requests.
  • To provide, schedule and support the requested computer, laptop, software, hardware, printer or network service.
  • To communicate about quotes, payments, service updates, refunds or customer support.
  • To maintain business, accounting, security and legal records.

3. Payment processing

Online payments are redirected to PayU's payment interface. Payment details are handled according to PayU's applicable privacy and security practices. We receive transaction-related information needed to identify and reconcile your payment.

4. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We may share necessary information with payment providers, hosting/service providers or authorities where required to operate the service, protect the website, comply with law or resolve a dispute. We retain information only for as long as reasonably required for these purposes and applicable legal obligations.

5. Device and data access

If you give us a device for diagnosis or repair, we will access files or settings only to the extent reasonably necessary for the requested work. Please back up important data before handing over a device. We will seek your approval before carrying out additional work outside the agreed scope.

6. Your choices and security

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to records we must retain by law. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
