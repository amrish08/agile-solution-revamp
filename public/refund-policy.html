Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy applies to payments made to Agile Solution for services booked through this website.

1. When a refund may be available

  • If Agile Solution cannot provide a paid service, the service amount paid for the undelivered service will be refunded.
  • If a customer requests cancellation before work has started and the request is accepted, the payment will generally be refunded in full.
  • If work has already started, any refund will be assessed based on the work completed, costs already incurred and the unused portion of the service fee.
  • If a duplicate payment or an incorrect amount is received, please contact us with the transaction details so we can verify and arrange any amount due.

2. Non-refundable or limited-refund situations

Charges for work already completed, parts or materials specifically ordered or used with the customer's approval, and third-party costs already incurred may not be refundable, to the extent permitted by law. This does not affect any statutory consumer rights.

3. How to request a refund

Email agilesolution2026@gmail.com or call +91 88259 85850. Include your name, registered mobile number, service description, payment date, amount and PayU transaction ID or payment reference. Do not send card details, UPI PIN, OTP or passwords.

4. Review and processing

We will review the request and communicate the outcome using your supplied contact details. Once a refund is approved and the transaction is verified, Agile Solution aims to initiate it to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. After initiation, the time taken for the credit to appear depends on PayU, the bank, card issuer or payment provider and may take additional time. We cannot control the bank or payment provider processing time.

5. Failed or pending payments

If your account is debited but the payment status shows failed, pending or incomplete, please contact us with the transaction reference. We will verify the status with the payment provider. If the transaction is confirmed as unsuccessful and a refund is due, the refund will be initiated after reconciliation in line with the payment provider and bank process. Duplicate or delayed debit cases may take additional time to reconcile.

6. Payment disputes

Please contact Agile Solution first so we can investigate and help resolve a payment or service concern. You may also use the applicable payment provider or consumer grievance channels.
