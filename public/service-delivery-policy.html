Service Delivery Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Agile Solution provides service-based computer and technology support. This website currently accepts service enquiries and bookings; it is not an online store for physical hardware products.

1. Service confirmation

After receiving your request, we will contact you using the phone number or email supplied to confirm the issue, service scope, applicable price and availability. A request is not a confirmed appointment until these details are agreed.

2. Scheduling and completion

Service timing depends on the type of issue, device condition, parts availability and workload. We will communicate the expected appointment or completion time when confirming the service. No fixed turnaround is promised unless expressly confirmed with you in writing.

3. Remote or in-person support

The mode of service (remote, collection, on-site or at an agreed service location) will be confirmed before work begins. Do not send a device or share remote-access credentials unless the arrangement has been confirmed directly with Agile Solution.

4. Parts and additional work

Replacement parts, accessories and work beyond the original scope are not included in a basic service fee unless expressly stated. We will obtain your approval for additional charges before proceeding. Part availability may affect completion time.

5. Digital deliverables

Where a service includes software setup, configuration, file transfer or other digital assistance, completion will be communicated by phone, email or the agreed service channel. Any third-party licence or subscription fee will be disclosed separately where applicable.

6. Customer support

For a booking update or service concern, contact us with your name and booking or payment reference. We will respond using the contact details provided.
